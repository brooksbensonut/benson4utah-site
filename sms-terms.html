How to Opt Out

Reply with any of these words at any time: STOP, QUIT, END, REVOKE, OPT OUT, CANCEL or UNSUBSCRIBE. You will receive one message confirming you are unsubscribed, and no further campaign texts after that.

You can also opt out by emailing brooks@benson4utah.com or calling (805) 746-6758 with the mobile number you want removed, or by any other reasonable means of telling us to stop. We honor every opt-out request promptly and no later than 10 business days after we receive it.

To rejoin after opting out, reply START or sign up again on our text sign-up page.

Eligibility and Carriers

You must be the mobile account holder or have the account holder's permission to opt in. Messages are available on major U.S. carriers. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Privacy

Text messaging opt-in data and consent are never shared with or sold to third parties. See our Privacy Policy for details.
