Meet Brooks Benson

New Energy for Utah Senate District 11

A 41-year-old business consultant bringing fresh perspective, proven problem-solving skills, and unwavering conservative values to represent the families of Eagle Mountain, Tooele, Herriman, Magna, and Kearns.

My Story

Like many of you, I moved to Utah seeking the quality of life that makes this state special. My wife and I chose to raise our family here because of Utah's values, community spirit, and the promise of wide-open spaces where children can grow up safely.

But over the years, I've watched that promise threatened by unmanaged growth. I've sat in the same traffic jams you have, frustrated as developers build houses without first ensuring the roads, schools, and infrastructure can handle the growth. I've seen my neighbors' commutes stretch to 60-90 minutes each way, stealing precious time from their families.

With over 18 years of professional experience spanning technology, energy infrastructure, and business operations, I've built my career on solving complex problems and delivering practical solutions. From managing natural gas pipeline operations to leading software development teams, I understand how to get things done efficiently and effectively.

Family & Values

My education in Behavioral Science - Family Studies from Utah Valley University isn't just academic training—it reflects my core belief that strong families are the foundation of strong communities. Every policy decision should be measured by one simple question: Does this strengthen or weaken families?

Faith & Family First

Our decisions in government should protect and strengthen the family unit, respect parental rights, and preserve the values that make Utah special.

Fiscal Responsibility

Government should live within its means, just like Utah families do. Every tax dollar should be spent wisely and with accountability to taxpayers.

Limited Government

The best solutions often come from communities, not bureaucracies. Government should enable, not obstruct, local problem-solving and individual freedom.

Constitutional Principles

Our Constitution and Bill of Rights aren't suggestions—they're the foundation of our freedom and must be defended at every level of government.
